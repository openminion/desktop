# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community.

Expected behavior includes respectful, professional communication; constructive
feedback; and keeping discussions focused on ideas and outcomes. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption
are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, chats, and
reviews. Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove participants or content that
violate it.

Report concerns through the maintainers' private contact method or request a
private reporting channel.
