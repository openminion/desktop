# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community.

Examples of expected behavior:

1. Be respectful and professional.
2. Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
3. Give and receive feedback constructively.
4. Help keep discussions focused and collaborative.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

1. Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
2. Threatening, abusive, or hateful language.
3. Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
4. Deliberate disruption, trolling, or bad-faith engagement.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, chats, and reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcement and may remove, edit, or reject
content or contributions that violate this policy.

Possible actions include:

1. warning,
2. temporary restriction, or
3. permanent removal from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to [hello@openminion.com](mailto:hello@openminion.com)
with the subject “Desktop conduct report”. Include the relevant project space,
what happened, and any supporting links.
